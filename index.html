01 · THE CHALLENGE

When skills exist, but the pathway is unclear.

The case study identifies a set of connected barriers between a beneficiary's lived experience and a relevant skilling or livelihood pathway.

01

From informal experience to structured opportunity.

Beneficiaries may have practical skills, aspirations and local preferences without knowing how these translate into formal qualification pathways, skill gaps or relevant opportunities.

◌

Language & Digital Access

Text-heavy workflows and language variation can increase the interaction burden for voice-preferred users.

⌁

Weak Skill Mapping

Informal skills and practical experience may be difficult to express using formal qualification terminology.

↔

Training–Livelihood Mismatch

Training recommendations need to reflect skills, interests, work preferences, mobility and context.

?

Skill-Gap Visibility

A useful recommendation should explain what is missing and what the beneficiary can do next.

⌖

Local Relevance

Opportunity information should be relevant to location and mobility constraints when reliable data is available.

↺

Follow-up Gap

The journey should not end at a recommendation; referral, training and outcome states matter too.

02 · THE SOLUTION

A voice-first livelihood decision-support system.

JeevanPath AI combines conversational AI with structured livelihood data, qualification references, deterministic rules and semantic matching.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

LLM-assisted. Domain-controlled.

The conversational layer helps understand and extract information. The domain workflow remains responsible for verification, matching, pathway logic and tracking.

07 · CASE STUDY

A synthetic beneficiary journey.

The case study uses a synthetic rural/semi-rural scenario to demonstrate how the workflow can translate lived experience into a structured pathway.

SYNTHETIC CASE SCENARIO

Practical experience, local preference, unclear formal pathway.

The beneficiary has studied to secondary level, has agriculture-linked practical experience, can operate basic agricultural equipment, is interested in technical skills and prefers a nearby livelihood opportunity.
